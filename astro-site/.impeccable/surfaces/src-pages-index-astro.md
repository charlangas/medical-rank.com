# Homepage and site shell

Scope: `/` and the shared layout every page inherits. Visitor mode: Persuade on the homepage; Read on guides, articles and hubs.
Audience and job: a doctor or practice operator deciding whether their marketing works and who to trust. Action: read the lead guide, or contact Medical Rank.
Proof on hand: one sourced guide with a worksheet. No testimonials, client figures or logos until the owner supplies them.
Constraints: current blue palette is pinned by the owner; conventional register chosen by the owner.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Medical Rank opens like the front page of a clinical journal: the best guide and its short answer lead, not a sales hero. It refuses the agency template of headline, button and stock photograph.

OWN-WORLD: White ground, deep blue #0023B0 as the only strong colour, bright blue #355FEC for interaction, pale blue #f3f6ff tint fields, ink #1c2233. A 3px blue masthead rule under the nameplate. Literata for headlines and long reading, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for interface and labels. Ruled index lists instead of cards; a tinted short-answer box; square corners.

STORY: The visitor sees a real question answered plainly, understands this is guidance written for the practice's side, finds their own decision in the index, and contacts Medical Rank when they want help.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Masthead with nameplate, topic links and a blue "Talk to us" button over the blue rule. Left, about 60% wide: the site's one-line purpose, then the featured guide's headline at display size, its summary, the tinted short-answer box and a "Read the guide" button. Right: a ruled index, "Decisions we help with", one row per topic.

FORM: Journal front page; the owner's pick from the safer re-roll (grounded candidate "medical journal article"). Seed key b8269c72. Signature interaction: the masthead rule draws in once on load; index rows shift their title into blue with an arrow on hover and focus.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
