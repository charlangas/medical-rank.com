# Homepage and site shell

Scope: `/` and the shared layout every page inherits. Visitor mode: Persuade on the homepage; Read on guides, articles and hubs.
Audience and job: US medical practice owners and marketing decision makers choosing an agency for local SEO or Google Ads. Primary action: talk about the practice. Resources support the service decision.
Proof on hand: one sourced guide with a worksheet. No testimonials, client figures or logos until the owner supplies them.
Constraints: current blue palette is pinned by the owner; conventional register chosen by the owner.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Medical Rank is an agency offering local SEO and Google Ads. The homepage names both services, explains the work and invites a conversation. The editorial experience lives under Resources.

OWN-WORLD: White ground, deep blue #0023B0 as the only strong colour, bright blue #355FEC for interaction, pale blue #f3f6ff tint fields, ink #1c2233. A 3px blue masthead rule under the nameplate. Literata for headlines and long reading, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for interface and labels. Ruled index lists instead of cards; a tinted short-answer box; square corners.

STORY: The visitor understands the audience and services, sees the scope and process, finds answers to buying questions, and starts a conversation. A small resource section demonstrates the agency's thinking.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Masthead with Local SEO, Google Ads, About and Resources links and a "Talk about your practice" button. A stable service headline leads: "Local SEO and Google Ads for medical practices." Supporting copy explains the agency role. An enquiry is the primary action; exploring the services is secondary.

FORM: Restrained agency homepage with editorial typography, a ruled two-service layout, a three-step delivery process and native FAQ disclosures. The masthead rule and resource-index interactions remain.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
